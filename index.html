MITRE ATT&CK techniques I have covered

How I investigate

One lab, written up the way I would hand it to a team lead: what happened, the evidence, my verdict, and what comes next.

My alert triage routine

  1. Scope the alert

    What fired, on which host and account, and when.

  2. Enrich

    Check the source in WHOIS, VirusTotal, Cisco Talos and Shodan.

  3. Correlate

    Pull related events in Splunk with stats, streamstats and transaction.

  4. Decide

    True or false positive, then escalate or close with a reason.

  5. Document

    Timeline, indicators and MITRE mapping in a formal incident report.
